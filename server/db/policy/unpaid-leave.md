title: Unpaid Leave
---

Employees who have exhausted all paid leave entitlements may apply for unpaid leave for up to 30 consecutive calendar days per year. Unpaid leave requires approval from the direct manager and HR. Applications must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance except in documented emergencies. During unpaid leave, health insurance and social insurance contributions continue but are calculated on the reduced salary. Unpaid leave longer than 30 days requires CEO or equivalent approval and is considered on a case-by-case basis. Unpaid leave does not count toward leave accrual for the month in which it falls.
