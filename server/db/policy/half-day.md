title: Half-Day Leave
---

All leave types (annual, sick, personal) can be taken as a half-day. The morning half is defined as the working hours before the lunch break (typically 08:00–12:00) and the afternoon half as the hours after (13:00–17:00). Half-day leaves are deducted as 0.5 days from the applicable leave balance. When requesting a half-day, employees must specify whether it is a morning or afternoon half. Half-day sick leave follows the same notification rules as full-day sick leave. Half-day requests are subject to manager approval and team coverage considerations.
