title: Public Holidays and Leave
---

Public holidays are not deducted from any leave balance — they are additional days off. The company observes all Vietnamese national public holidays as defined by the Labor Law (approximately 11 days per year), including New Year's Day (1 Jan), Vietnamese New Year/Tet (3–5 days), Hung Kings Commemoration (10/3 lunar), Liberation Day (30 Apr), International Labor Day (1 May), National Day (2–3 Sep), and Christmas (25 Dec, optional). When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is taken as a substitute day off. Leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday follows standard notice-period rules.
