title: Annual Leave Entitlement
---

Full-time employees are entitled to 12 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1 day per month starting from the first day of employment. Employees who join after the 15th of the month do not accrue leave for that partial month. Annual leave may be taken in full-day or half-day increments. Employees should request annual leave at least 3 business days in advance for short requests (1–2 days) and at least 2 weeks in advance for requests of 3 or more consecutive days.
