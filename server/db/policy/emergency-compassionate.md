title: Emergency and Compassionate Leave
---

Employees are entitled to up to 3 days of paid compassionate leave per event for: the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law); the critical illness requiring hospitalization of a spouse or child; a natural disaster or fire directly affecting the employee's home. Additional days beyond the 3-day entitlement may be taken as annual or unpaid leave. Evidence (e.g., death certificate, hospital admission paper) must be provided within 5 business days. Emergency leave for unexpected urgent situations is evaluated case-by-case. Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible and submit documentation upon return.
